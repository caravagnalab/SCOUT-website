Why SCOUT matters

SCOUT reflects the structured complexity of cancer evolution, not isolated synthetic perturbations.

Cancer genomes are coupled systems
Variant allele frequencies, copy-number states, mutational signatures, and phylogenetic structure arise jointly from evolutionary processes.
Beyond spike-ins
Evaluates methods using explicit evolutionary ground truth rather than simplified perturbation-based benchmarks.
Clinical WGS standards
Supports reproducible interpretation standards for whole-genome sequencing in precision oncology.
Mechanistic benchmarking
Enables evaluation against clonal history, spatial bias, treatment selection, and genome-generating mechanisms.
